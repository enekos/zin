Actions and scenarios
================================================================================

action deposit(amount is an integer from 1 to 5)
  requires status is open
  requires balance + amount is at most 20
  balance becomes balance + amount

fair action work(worker is a worker)
  at of worker becomes if ready then running else waiting

strongly fair action finish(worker is a worker)
  at[worker] becomes done

scenario "Deposit, withdraw, and close"
  deposit with amount = 5
  deposit(3)
  reject reopen
  expect balance is 8 and status is open
